Our LegacyThe Art of Bespoke Tailoring

For over six decades, Kapoor Matching Centre has been a cornerstone of trust and quality for women's traditional tailoring.

Founded in 1960, we have stayed committed to the perfect fit. Every suit and designer blouse is stitched to match your unique silhouette and style preferences. We understand that matching matching materials like astar (lining) and saree falls is an art, which is why we source the finest accessories to complement your expensive garments.
